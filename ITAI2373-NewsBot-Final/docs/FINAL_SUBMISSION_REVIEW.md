## Verified results and interpretation

[CI run 45679903622](https://github.com/jtuozzo1616/Final-Jose-Group-NewsBot-Intelligence-System/actions/runs/45679903622) for revision 572edc9e6d9ac9c50f2d70315f57712e1e09d9c8 completed successfully: 27 tests passed, 1 warning; CLI, research and artifact upload succeeded. [Subsequent CI run 45680349376](https://github.com/jtuozzo1616/Final-Jose-Group-NewsBot-Intelligence-System/actions/runs/45680349376) also succeeded.

| Synthetic feature set | Accuracy | Macro-F1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Word TF-IDF baseline | 0.5667 | 0.5371 |
| Pivot word TF-IDF | 0.6000 | 0.5720 |
| Pivot + character n-grams | 0.6667 | 0.6143 |

Paired comparisons used the same 30 fictional records and stratified splits, with features fitted inside each fold. The absolute F1 gain was 0.0772. The 24 selected retrieval probes obtained MRR@3 and Recall@3 1.0 with pivot and 0.625 without it; this is not an independent retrieval benchmark. Leave-one-language-out diagnostics are generated by the research runner and included in the CI artifact.

The [historical midterm](../../MIDTERM.md) separately achieved 96.18% accuracy and 0.9605 macro-F1 on 288 BBC articles from 2004–2005. Do not use that number as NewsBot 2.0 accuracy. The low synthetic business/entertainment recall and lexicon dependence justify external, publisher- and time-separated testing.

Confirm the official group name, individual-submission approval, attribution requirements and any requirements in the separate Canvas downloads. A high grade or bonus cannot be guaranteed. No production deployment, fully validated neural generation or actual optional translation/embedding execution is claimed.
